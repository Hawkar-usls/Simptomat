**Subject:** Research data availability inquiry — timing, temporal order, attention and memory in AD/SCD/MCI

Dear Dr. Teghil,

I am preparing an independent, non-commercial research-methods evaluation of how temporal processing measures relate to memory and attention across cognitive-aging and Alzheimer's-disease cohorts, prompted by your 2023 study.

Your published data-availability statement notes that the underlying dataset may be available on reasonable request. Would it be possible to obtain the data dictionary and details of the conditions for accessing de-identified participant-level scores, subject to any required ethics approvals and data-use agreements?

We are interested in verifying whether the following outcomes can be linked *within the same participant and session*: (1) task-level attention/engagement or behavioral response logs; (2) immediate item recognition or immediate recall, clearly distinguished; (3) direct temporal-order judgments of experimentally experienced events, if collected, rather than only dating of public events or duration judgments; and (4) delayed episodic recall, ideally with stimulus identifiers and documented intervals. We would also welcome non-identifying information on subjective passage-of-time reports, demographics/clinical-group definition, study exclusions, trial timing and task protocols.

If some of these variables were not collected, a confirmation of which measures are available would be valuable. We will not presume the existence of unreported measures or treat a partial dataset as sufficient evidence for a temporal-consciousness mechanism.

We would use the data only under the permission and privacy conditions agreed with your institution, with an externally declared analysis plan, cohort/participant-disjoint validation and reporting of negative results. We would not publish individual-level data or offer medical diagnoses. We would be pleased to provide the full analysis protocol for review before any transfer.

Thank you for considering this inquiry.
